# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People planning, performing, and tracking strength workouts. They use Just Workout to decide what they can realistically train each week, then turn those choices into a Training Plan they can follow.

## Product Purpose

Just Workout helps users create a Plan Blueprint, generate a Training Plan, and track strength training progress without needing to understand programming terminology first.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, practical.

## Anti-references

Avoid generic gym-bro aesthetics, aggressive transformation marketing, and naming that conflicts with the domain language in `CONTEXT.md`.

## Design Principles

Respect the builder flow: each screen should make the current choice, its constraints, and the next step obvious.

Show recommendations with reasons: a Recommended Default should explain why it fits without blocking compatible alternatives.

Keep training language consistent: use Plan Blueprint, Training Plan, Training Frequency, Training Split, and Rep Range Style precisely.

Design for task focus: keep the primary choice, selected-state details, and blueprint status visible without unnecessary desktop scrolling.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Preserve keyboard-accessible form controls, visible focus states, semantic headings, reduced-motion compatibility, and layouts that remain usable from laptop screens through large desktop displays.
